🔬 About the Material

Introduction

This interactive platform was developed to support the teaching of the immunological mechanisms of allergies in undergraduate Medicine. The material brings together four components — illustrated handout, slides with speaking notes, interactive exercises and a clinical case study — in a single web application, with a medical layout (blue, yellow and white) and smooth navigation.

Alignment with the 2025 Medicine DCN

This material has been updated in line with CNE/CES Resolution No. 3 of 30 September 2025, which establishes the National Curriculum Guidelines (DCN) for the Undergraduate Medicine Course, revoking CNE/CES Resolution No. 3/2014 [5]. The full text of the new DCN was consulted for curricular alignment.

The 2025 DCN establishes that the physician must be a professional with general, solid, critical and reflective training, able to work at all levels of health care, with care centered on the person, the family and the community (Arts. 2–4). The new DCN lists 27 competencies (Art. 8, items I–XXVII). This material directly addresses the following competencies:

Scientific Integrity Protocol

For each factual statement in this material, the following criteria were observed:

  1. Preferred primary sources: internationally recognized textbooks (Abbas; Janeway/Murphy; Robbins); guidelines from scientific societies (WAO, GINA); peer-reviewed articles in indexed journals; official normative documents (MEC/CNE, Ministry of Health) — including the full text of CNE/CES Res. No. 3/2025.
  2. Triple verification: each key concept confirmed in at least two independent sources.
  3. Exclusion of unverified sources: no information from blogs, wikis, social networks or content without identifiable scientific authorship was used.
  4. Traceability: each statement linked to a numbered citation [n].
  5. Currency: recent editions (2022/2023) of the textbooks and the latest versions of the guidelines.

International Best Teaching Practices

  • Revised Bloom's Taxonomy (Anderson & Krathwohl, 2001): learning objectives structured by cognitive level — Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, Create.
  • Active methodologies (DCN 2025, Art. 18): case study with problematization, in small groups, with progressive ("sliced") reasoning.
  • Programmatic assessment (DCN 2025, Art. 36): continuous feedback with mandatory individual feedback, covering the three domains of competence — cognitive, psychomotor and attitudinal.
  • Teaching–service–community integration (DCN 2025, Art. 22): clinical case set in primary care/ESF, with public health actions.
  • Interactive exercises with immediate feedback: the platform offers instant checking of multiple-choice answers, with an explanatory comment.
